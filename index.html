    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is Rewritr really free?

    Yes — completely. No account, no credit card, no hidden limits for normal use.

    Do I need to sign in?

    Nope. You can use everything as a guest. Your recent improvements are saved locally in your own browser.

    Is my text stored anywhere?

    Your text is sent to the AI to generate the rewrite, but Rewritr doesn't keep a copy. History lives only in your browser's local storage.

    Which platforms does it support?

    AI mode covers ChatGPT, Claude and Midjourney. Social mode covers LinkedIn, X and Instagram — with more on the way.
